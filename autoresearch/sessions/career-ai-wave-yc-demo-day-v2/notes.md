## Insights
- **Agent Market Evolution:** As demonstrated across recent YC batches, generic agent infrastructure is commoditized; value and step-function contract revenue have migrated to vertical AI agents and AI-native full-stack operators in unsexy domains (insurance, trusts, logistics).
- **Four-Path Hierarchy:** For an engineer with Staff-level execution (10 yr Google, Lyft, LangGraph) but zero public footprint and 6-12 months runway:
  - *Path C (Pure Research)* is disqualified by compute concentration (TPUs, frontier hyperscaler clusters) and multi-year publishing cycles.
  - *Path B (Solo Startup)* has high potential but risks runway exhaustion if initiated cold before customer validation.
  - *Path A (Major Lab)* requires public proof-of-work to bypass credentialist screening.
  - *Path D (Inbound Leverage & Staged Validation)* is the highest EV: creating public production artifacts while employed creates competing optionality between Path B (funded startup) and Path A (Staff lab role).
- **Runway Calibration & Decision Gates:** With 6-12 months of runway, Ivan cannot afford unhedged exploratory burn. A strict "runway gate" must be maintained—retaining salary until either customer validation ($10k MRR / signed LOIs) or two Tier-1 inbound interview loops are unlocked.
- **Opportunity Cost Discipline:** Reclaiming 15-20 weekly hours for 90-day milestones requires explicit stop-doing commitments: shedding non-essential internal Lyft committees, deprioritizing uncompensated mentoring, and abandoning private, non-public side tinkering.
