# Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 (absent or harmful) to 10 (could not be better), integers only; the total is the sum over all dimensions. Core dimensions are fixed by the engine. Mission-specific dimensions were drafted for this session's mission and approved at checkpoint 0. Only the reviewer changes this file afterwards.

## Core dimensions

### specificity: Specificity

Each step names a concrete action, its object and, where possible, its owner and a first deadline. A 10 has no step that could be pasted into an unrelated plan; a 1 is generic advice.

### grounding: Grounding in the transcript

Each step traces to something specific in the source document (a quoted claim, number or exchange) and the link is stated. A 10 grounds every step; a 1 could have been written without reading the transcript.

### mission_fit: Mission fit

The steps, taken together, move the stated mission forward and nothing is off-target. A 10 covers the mission's main levers and risks; a 1 answers a different question.

### actionability: Actionability and sequencing

The steps can be started now with the resources the mission implies, dependencies are ordered, and the first step is unambiguous. A 10 is a runnable plan; a 1 needs another planning pass.

### insight: Non-obvious insight

The recommendations surface something a careful plain read of the transcript would miss: a gap, a contradiction, a leverage point the decomposition exposed. A 10 changes what you would do next; a 1 restates the transcript.

## Mission-specific dimensions

### comparative_path_ranking: Comparative Path Ranking

A 10 systematically evaluates, contrasts, and ranks all four required paths (AI lab role, vertical startup, research track, wealth/optionality) against Ivan's specific profile before justifying the highest-EV choice. A 1 focuses on a single pre-selected path or omits comparative evaluation across the four alternatives.

### runway_risk_discipline: Runway Risk Discipline

A 10 directly accounts for Ivan's 6-to-12-month runway constraint by including concrete financial de-risking checkpoints and conversion triggers before or upon leaving salaried employment. A 1 ignores the runway limit or prescribes speculative, high-burn efforts that cannot plausibly generate offers or revenue within 12 months.

### subtractive_focus: Subtractive Focus

A 10 explicitly specifies concrete tasks, commitments, or engineering habits Ivan must actively deprioritize or stop doing at Lyft to create the required bandwidth. A 1 simply piles on new responsibilities and milestones without identifying what current work to shed.
