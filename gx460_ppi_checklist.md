# GX 460 — Phone PPI Checklist (no tools, no crawling)

2019 GX 460 Premium/KDSS · VIN JTJBM7FX3K5218253 · seller claims ~70k mi

## Before you go (5 min, at home)
- [ ] nhtsa.gov/recalls + VIN → expect **0 open**
- [ ] Ask seller: cold engine, parked on flat spot, title in hand

## 1 · Stance & lean (3 min)
- [ ] 20 ft back, dead-center front then rear: body even left/right, roof parallel to ground
- [ ] Fingers in tire-to-fender gap, all 4 wheels: **left = right (±1 finger), rear ≈ front + 1**
- [ ] Driver side low at both axles → KDSS reset, negotiate (not a walk)
- [ ] Push each corner down 3×: one rebound, then stops. Keeps bobbing = worn shocks

## 2 · KDSS leak peek (3 min, kneel + phone light)
- [ ] Driver-side frame rail under front door → valve block: **dry dusty = OK, wet/oily = WALK**
- [ ] Each front wheel well, light toward sway bar: cylinder + lines dry
- [ ] Behind each rear tire: rear cylinder dry
- [ ] Pavement under truck: no fresh spots

## 3 · Body & tires (4 min)
- [ ] Panel gaps even; black paint matches panel-to-panel; no overspray on trim/rubber (report says no accidents — verify)
- [ ] All 4 tires **265/60R18**, same brand; tread even inner vs outer; palm across tread — no saw-tooth
- [ ] Sidewall DOT date: older than ~2021 → budget tires
- [ ] Through wheel spokes: visible pad meat, no big rotor lip

## 4 · Engine bay (3 min)
- [ ] Dipstick: amber/brown OK · **milky or glittery = WALK**
- [ ] Coolant reservoir pink, at line; no white crust at radiator
- [ ] Brake fluid at line; belt no cracks; battery (4/2022) no fuzz
- [ ] No raw-fuel or burnt smell

## 5 · Cabin (4 min)
- [ ] Odometer ≈ 70k; wear matches: pedal rubber, wheel shine, seat bolster
- [ ] Start: all dash lights come on, then ALL go out (airbag, 4WD, check engine)
- [ ] AC ice cold; all windows, sunroof, locks, mirrors, cameras; both fobs
- [ ] Side-swing rear door + glass hatch latch clean
- [ ] Fold 2nd/3rd rows; sniff for musty, look for water stains

## 6 · Test drive (15 min) — do not buy without it
- [ ] Cold start: no rattle, no smoke
- [ ] Lot: full-lock circles both ways (no clicks) · slow figure-8, windows down (no clunks) · diagonal over driveway lip (no clunk)
- [ ] Speed bump: one settled motion, no float
- [ ] Straight road: wheel centered, no pull
- [ ] On-ramp sweep: stays **flat** (KDSS working); wallowy = system low
- [ ] 70 mph: no shake · firm brake: straight, no shudder
- [ ] Shifts smooth, no clunky downshifts
- [ ] Lot: stop, shift N, twist knob to L4, feel it engage, back to H4
- [ ] After: sniff bay, check ground for new drips

## 7 · Paperwork (5 min) — AutoCheck flags to clear
- [ ] Title = **duplicate issued ~06/29/2026 with lien** → ask why duplicate + is loan paid off?
- [ ] If lien open: pay at their **lender/bank**, get payoff + lien release. Never cash in driveway
- [ ] Seller photo ID = name on title/registration
- [ ] Last reported odometer **50,942 (12/2023)** → ask for any 2024–26 service receipts
- [ ] Ask: what was "body service" 8/2023? · 2022 re-lien = refi? · what did hitch tow? · why selling?
- [ ] Fuel-pump recall likely done 4/2021 — confirm with Lexus by VIN

## Verdict rules
**WALK:** wet KDSS parts · milky oil · crash evidence · seller ≠ title name · won't do lender payoff · odometer far off 70k
**NEGOTIATE:** driver-side lean · old/worn tires · no records since 2023 · 60k service not done
**BUY signal:** everything above clean — history is 1-owner CA, no accidents, dealer-maintained to 51k
